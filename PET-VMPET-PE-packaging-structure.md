# PET/VMPET/PE Flexible Packaging Structure

PET/VMPET/PE is a common flexible packaging material structure used when products need better moisture, light and oxygen barrier than general PET/PE structures.

It is often used for food packaging, coffee packaging, snack packaging, powder packaging, candy packaging and other products that need improved barrier performance.

## What Is PET/VMPET/PE?

PET/VMPET/PE usually means:

- PET outer printing layer
- VMPET metallized barrier layer
- PE inner heat sealing layer

PET supports printing appearance and structure.

VMPET provides improved light barrier and barrier performance compared with non-metallized film.

PE provides heat sealing performance.

The final material specification should be confirmed according to product type, filling weight, shelf life requirement, storage condition and packing method.

## Common Applications

PET/VMPET/PE may be used for:

- Coffee packaging
- Snack packaging
- Biscuit packaging
- Candy packaging
- Powder packaging
- Seasoning packaging
- Pet food packaging
- Stand up zipper pouches
- Flat bottom pouches
- Food packaging roll film
- Kraft paper laminated bags

For some products requiring very high oxygen or aroma barrier, aluminum foil structures may be considered after testing.

## Why Use VMPET?

VMPET is often used when buyers need better barrier performance than basic transparent structures.

Possible reasons include:

- Better light barrier
- Improved moisture barrier
- Improved oxygen barrier compared with simple structures
- Better shelf life support
- Suitable for products sensitive to moisture or aroma loss
- Common in coffee, snack and dry food packaging

VMPET is not the same as aluminum foil. If the product requires a stronger barrier, PET/AL/PE or other high-barrier structures may need to be reviewed.

## Points to Confirm

Before choosing PET/VMPET/PE, buyers should confirm:

1. Product type
2. Filling weight
3. Shelf life requirement
4. Moisture barrier requirement
5. Oxygen barrier requirement
6. Light barrier requirement
7. Bag type or roll film type
8. Thickness
9. Quantity
10. Printing colors
11. Storage condition
12. Destination market
