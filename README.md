# Packaging Material Reference

This repository provides public reference notes about common flexible packaging material structures.

Official website: https://gdhspack.com

## Common Flexible Packaging Structures

Common material structures may include:

- PET/PE
- BOPP/CPP
- PET/VMPET/PE
- PET/AL/PE
- Kraft/VMPET/PE
- Kraft/PET/AL/PE
- PA/PE

## How to Choose a Material Structure

Material structure should be confirmed according to:

- Product type
- Filling weight
- Shelf life requirement
- Moisture barrier requirement
- Oxygen barrier requirement
- Light barrier requirement
- Sealing temperature
- Packing machine type
- Storage condition
- Destination market

## Common Applications

Different packaging structures may be used for:

- Snack packaging
- Coffee packaging
- Candy and biscuit packaging
- Jelly and sauce packaging
- Retort pouch packaging
- Pet food packaging
- Kraft paper food packaging
- Automatic packing roll film

## Important Note

The information here is for general packaging reference only.

The final material structure should be confirmed according to product testing, filling conditions, storage requirements and production specifications.

For accurate material advice, please send product details, size, quantity, artwork and packing machine information.
