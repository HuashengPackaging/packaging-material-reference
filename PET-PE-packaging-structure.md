# PET/PE Flexible Packaging Structure

PET/PE is a common flexible packaging material structure used for many food packaging applications.

It combines PET as the outer printing layer and PE as the inner heat sealing layer. The structure can be used for many dry food, snack, powder, seasoning and general flexible packaging projects.

Official website: https://gdhspack.com

## What Is PET/PE?

PET/PE usually means:

- PET outer layer
- Printing or reverse printing layer
- PE inner sealing layer

PET provides printability, stiffness and surface appearance.

PE provides heat sealing performance and direct sealing function.

The final material specification should be confirmed according to product type, thickness, packing machine and shelf life requirement.

## Common Applications

PET/PE may be used for:

- Snack packaging
- Biscuit packaging
- Candy packaging
- Powder packaging
- Seasoning packaging
- Three side seal pouches
- Stand up zipper pouches
- Food packaging roll film
- General dry food packaging

For products requiring higher moisture, oxygen or light barrier, metallized film or aluminum foil structures may be considered.

## Advantages

Possible advantages of PET/PE include:

- Good printing appearance
- Flexible structure
- Good heat sealing performance
- Suitable for many dry food products
- Suitable for custom printing
- Available for roll film or pre-made pouches

## Points to Confirm

Before choosing PET/PE, buyers should confirm:

1. Product type
2. Filling weight
3. Shelf life requirement
4. Moisture barrier requirement
5. Oxygen barrier requirement
6. Film thickness
7. Packing machine type
8. Sealing temperature
9. Quantity
10. Storage condition

## PET/PE for Roll Film

PET/PE can be used for food packaging roll film when the sealing layer and film stiffness match the buyer's automatic packing machine.

For roll film projects, buyers should confirm:

- Roll width
- Thickness
- Eye mark position
- Roll direction
- Film tension
- Sealing temperature
- Machine speed
- Cutting accuracy requirement
